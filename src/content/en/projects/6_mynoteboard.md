This project realized a note-taking application for _Windows 8 (RT)_ that utilized the GPU to render strokes on a virtual piece of paper.
For the rendering part a _DirectX_ wrapper called [SharpDX][sharpdx] was used in combination with _C#_. The application allowed the user to pair their
device with others via _Bluetooth_, _Wi-Fi_ or over the network to work together on one piece of paper.

[sharpdx]: http://sharpdx.org/
