## Frequently asked questions

### I bought "Remove ads" but I'm seeing ads again.

Open the settings in the game and tap "Restore purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you made the purchase with.

### What exactly does "Remove ads" do?

Banners and interstitial ads are gone for good. You can still watch the optional videos to revive or double your coins if you want to.

### How does the daily river work?

Every day there's a new river that is the same for everyone; it changes at midnight UTC. Your best run goes on the daily leaderboard, and you race the ghost of the next-best player. The server replays every run from your steering inputs before it counts.

### Where is my progress stored? Can I move it to another device?

Coins, canoes, achievements and progress are stored only on your device. They're gone if you delete the app, and moving them to another device isn't possible at the moment. You can always restore the "Remove ads" purchase.

### How do I change my leaderboard name?

In the settings, under "Player name".

### How do I delete my data?

In the settings, under "Delete my data". Your name and your daily runs are then deleted from the server; coins, canoes and progress on your device are kept. More in the [privacy policy](/apps/paddle-perfect/privacy).

### There's an inappropriate name or a suspicious score on the leaderboard.

Tap the entry on the leaderboard and choose "Report player" – or send me an email.

### What's the best way to steer?

In the settings you can choose between tilt, finger steering (the canoe follows your finger) and relative touch steering (the canoe copies your swipe, your finger can be anywhere). With tilt, the way you hold the phone when you set off counts as straight – so hold it at the start the way you want to play.
