I was part of the [eOCS](https://www.eurofunk.com/products/command-control/eocs) project at eurofunk, which offers a **digital command center solution** for emergency services.
The frontend is **Angular**, backed by a Spring backend. I broke features down, worked out the technical and **UX concepts** behind them, built them, and guarded **code quality** in merge reviews.
One of my favourite tasks was building custom form controls and reusable components in general.
I also kept the **software architecture** sustainable by modularising it and enforcing boundaries between those modules, and spent time **mentoring** and training colleagues.
On-site I analysed bugs and system behaviour — **rendering performance** and tracking down memory leaks were a particular focus.
