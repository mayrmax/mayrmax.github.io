I worked on the concept and development of an **IoT stack** built around the [Digital Twin Definition Language](https://github.com/Azure/opendigitaltwins-dtdl), written in **.NET** on top of [Microsoft Orleans](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/orleans/).
Users could define their own vocabulary and build a **knowledge graph** from it, then query and maintain that graph through a range of APIs and applications.
Alongside the backend I led the web side, developed concepts for where the stack should go next, and contributed to working groups such as the Self-Description working group of [GAIA-X](https://www.gaia-x.eu/).
