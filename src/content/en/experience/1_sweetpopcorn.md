I co-run [SweetPopcorn.Studio](https://sweetpopcorn.studio/), where we build software for companies that need more than a template.
Our products are [routero](https://www.routero.eu/), a platform for the **Route Compatibility Check** that lets operators carry out an RCC quickly, document it properly and sign it off, and [kkmOS](https://kkmos.eu/), a **crisis and disaster management** platform used by authorities and emergency services.
Both products live on geodata, so a large part of the work has been building up real depth in **GIS** — from map layers to OGC interfaces.
My part spans the whole stack, from the **architecture** down to the implementation, and from the **.NET backend** to the frontend.
