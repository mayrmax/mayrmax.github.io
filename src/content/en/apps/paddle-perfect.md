One river. One life. How far can you go? Steer your canoe with your finger or by tilting the phone, dodge rocks, logs and animals, and hit the exact centre of the gates to build Perfect streaks. Every river is built to be fair: every obstacle can be cleared if you paddle cleanly.

- **Twelve worlds, four of them secret** – from forest, jungle and canyon to the ice river, volcano and a dark cave.
- **Daily river** – a new river every day, the same for everyone, with a daily leaderboard and the ghost of the next-best player to race.
- **Missions, achievements and 9 canoes** to unlock, plus hats, wake trails and Perfect effects.

The game's simulation is plain, deterministic C#. The server runs the very same code to replay every daily run from its steering inputs, so no score on the leaderboard has to be taken on trust.

Free with ads; a one-time purchase removes them for good.
