## 2. Overview

You can play Paddle Perfect without an account, email address or sign-in.

- **Stored only on your device:** progress, best scores, coins, canoes, missions, achievements and settings. This data never leaves your device; uninstalling the app deletes it.
- **Sent to my server** only if you play the daily river with its leaderboard (section 3) or agree to anonymous gameplay statistics (section 4).
- **Sent to Unity** so that ads can be shown (section 6). After the "Remove ads" purchase, no ads are loaded.

The app does not use location, contacts, camera or microphone, and on iOS it does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

## 3. Daily river and leaderboard

When you play the daily river and join the leaderboard, you choose a player name. I process:

- your player name, which is visible to all players on the daily leaderboard (please don't use your real full name);
- a random player ID and a secret access token that stays on your device (the server stores only a hash of it);
- your daily runs: date, your steering inputs per game step, score, duration, cause of the crash and app version.

**Purpose:** the server replays every run from your inputs to keep the leaderboard fair, and shows the leaderboard. The day's best run may be shown to others as a "ghost" (a replay of your run with your player name). **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, because you are using a feature you requested.

**Moderation:** player names are checked automatically for offensive or prohibited terms. Other players can report an entry; after several reports it is hidden and reviewed by me. I may change inappropriate names and lock or delete entries. For this I process who reported whom and when. **Legal basis:** my legitimate interest in a safe, fair leaderboard (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Retention and deletion:** under _Settings → Delete my data_ you can immediately delete your player name, player ID, all daily runs and reports from the server at any time. Otherwise the data is kept for as long as the leaderboard exists. Deleted data disappears from the daily backups after 14 days at the latest.

## 4. Anonymous gameplay statistics (only with consent)

On first launch the app asks whether you want to send anonymous gameplay statistics. Only if you agree, it sends events such as app start, start and end of a run (duration, world reached, cause of crash, score), completed missions and achievements, in-game shop purchases made with game coins, and error and crash reports. These are sent together with a random installation ID, the app version and the platform (iOS/Android). No name, player ID, advertising ID or location is sent.

**Purpose:** improving the game, for example tuning the difficulty and finding bugs. **Legal basis:** your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). **Retention:** events are deleted automatically after 90 days. You can withdraw your consent at any time under _Settings → Send anonymous gameplay statistics_; from then on no data is sent. When you delete your data (section 3), the app also gets a new installation ID.

## 5. Server and hosting

My server is operated by Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany, in a data centre in Nuremberg. Hetzner acts as my processor (Art. 28 GDPR), and the data stays in the EU. All connections are encrypted with HTTPS.

For every request the server has to process your IP address to answer it and to prevent abuse (a limit on requests per minute). The app's interface keeps the IP address only briefly in memory for this. Technical access logs of the upstream web server (IP address, time, requested address) are kept for no more than 30 days. **Legal basis:** my legitimate interest in operating the service securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## 6. Advertising (Unity Ads)

The free version shows ads via Unity Ads, provided by Unity Technologies SF, 30 3rd Street, San Francisco, CA 94103, USA, and its affiliates ("Unity"). The ads are a banner in the menu and on the end screen, an occasional full-screen ad, and optional video ads (for example to revive or to double your coins). To deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure reach, Unity processes device and usage information, such as device model, operating system, language, IP address, a device ID assigned by Unity, and data about the ads shown and your interaction with them. Unity is the controller for this processing; see [Unity's privacy policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).

- **iOS:** the app does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). The IDFA is not used and no personalised ads are shown.
- **Android:** personalised ads, including the use of your device's advertising ID, are shown only with your consent. The app asks on first launch, and you can change your choice at any time under _Settings → Personalised ads_. Without consent, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in your Android settings.

**Legal basis:** for personalised ads, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); otherwise my legitimate interest in funding the free app through advertising (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA; Unity relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## 7. Purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), and their privacy policies apply. I receive no payment data, only the confirmation of the purchase. It is stored on your device and checked again when you use "Restore purchases". Technically this runs through Unity In-App Purchasing. **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract).

## 8. Children

Paddle Perfect is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children. Children under 14 should agree to personalised ads and gameplay statistics only with their parents' permission, and should not use their real name as player name. Parents can delete the data in the app at any time or write to me.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent at any time with effect for the future.

Because the app works without an account, I can only match data to you if you tell me your player ID or make the request from within the app (for example via _Delete my data_). To make a request, write to max(dot)mayr@gmail.com.

If you believe the processing of your data violates data protection law, you can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Austria this is the [Datenschutzbehörde](https://www.dsb.gv.at/), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna.
