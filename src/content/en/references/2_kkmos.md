A platform for authorities, emergency services and companies handling the incidents that fall outside routine operations — natural disasters, major accidents, mass gatherings. It combines a **digital situation map**, an **incident logbook** and staff management, with reports passing between state, district and municipal level.

Residents can file reports with photo and location via QR code, every change is logged with timestamp and user, and an open **OGC interface** connects it to existing GIS systems, while **webhooks** feed events to the systems around it. It runs on-premise or in an EU cloud, so it keeps working when the network does not. Built at [SweetPopcorn.Studio](https://sweetpopcorn.studio/).
