My own startup, built from scratch: authentic recipes from around the world, with the regional context that usually gets lost. Over 10,000 people visit every day, all of them through **organic search**, and it all runs on **a single server**: **Domain-Driven Design** on **PostgreSQL**, search directly in the database behind its own caching layer, and an **Astro** frontend served through **Bunny CDN**.
